Negative reviews

The angry 1-star about service

Use when: staff behavior is the complaint. Goal: de-escalate + show readers you care.

Hi [Name], thank you for this feedback — I'm sorry your visit fell short, especially around service. That's not the standard we hold ourselves to, and I'm reviewing what happened with the team that day. I'd genuinely appreciate the chance to make it right; please reach me directly at [phone/email]. — [Owner name], [Business]

Cold food / long wait complaint

Use when: product or speed issue. Goal: own it without over-explaining.

[Name], thanks for telling us — a cold plate and a long wait is a fair reason to be frustrated, and we apologize. We've flagged this with the kitchen and are tightening ticket times during peak hours. We hope you'll give us another shot so we can show you our usual standard. — [Owner], [Business]

You believe the review is fake or mistaken

Use when: you have no record of the visit. Stay factual and courteous — future customers are the audience.

Thanks for taking the time to leave feedback. We take reviews seriously and checked our records for [date] but couldn't match the details described to a visit. If we're mistaken, please contact us at [email] with more information — we'd like to resolve it. In any case, we wish you well.

Accusation of overcharging / billing dispute

Use when: money dispute. Never litigate numbers publicly; invite details privately.

Hi [Name], I want to look into this immediately — that's not the experience we intend at any price point. Could you email [contact] with your receipt or visit date? If we made an error, we'll correct it and make it right. — [Owner], [Business]

Health / cleanliness complaint (serious)

Use when: hygiene alleged. Speed and seriousness matter more than persuasion.

[Name], thank you for raising this — we treat cleanliness complaints with the utmost seriousness. Our kitchen passed its most recent inspection on [date], but I've asked our team to re-review protocols today. Please contact me directly at [phone] so I can understand exactly what you saw. — [Owner], [Business]

Mixed & lukewarm reviews

The 3-star "good but…"

Use when: praise + one gripe. Thank first, fix second.

Thank you, [Name] — so glad you enjoyed [specific thing they praised]. You're right about [their criticism]; we're on it, and it should be noticeably better on your next visit. We appreciate you taking the time to help us improve. — [Team at Business]

Positive reviews

Warm thank-you (the SEO-friendly reply)

Use when: happy customer. Reinforce what they loved — keywords help local ranking.

Wow, thank you [Name]! Reviews like this make our week. We'll pass your kind words to [staff member/team] — they'll be thrilled. Next time, try [related item/service]; we think you'll love it too. — Everyone at [Business]

Brief & confident

Use when: short rave review, no questions raised.

Thanks so much, [Name] — see you next time! — [Business]
